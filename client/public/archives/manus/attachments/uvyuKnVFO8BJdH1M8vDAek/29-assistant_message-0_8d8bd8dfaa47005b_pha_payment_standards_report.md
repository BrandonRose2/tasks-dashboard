# Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher — PHA Payment Standards
## Apartment Corp — All 43 Properties

> **Source:** Individual Public Housing Authority (PHA) payment standard schedules, researched from official PHA websites and HUD records.
> **Note:** Payment Standards are the maximum monthly subsidy a Housing Authority will pay toward rent + utilities under the Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) program. These differ from HUD Fair Market Rents (FMRs) — each PHA sets its own standards, typically between 90%–110% of the FMR. Where a specific PHA standard could not be confirmed, the HUD FMR is used as a fallback (marked with ⚠).

## Notes

**⚠ = HUD FMR used as fallback** — The specific PHA payment standard could not be confirmed from an official source. The HUD Fair Market Rent is shown instead as the best available estimate.

**Important:** Payment standards can change annually. Always verify current rates directly with the local PHA before signing contracts or making financial projections.

**Data Sources:** Individual PHA websites, Louisiana Housing Corporation, Texas TDHCA, and HUD FMR Schedule.

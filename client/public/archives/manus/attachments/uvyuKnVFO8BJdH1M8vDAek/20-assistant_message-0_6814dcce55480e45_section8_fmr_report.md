## Summary Notes

**Important Distinctions:**

- **Fair Market Rents (FMRs)** are set at the county or metro area level by HUD. All properties in the same metro area share the same FMR, regardless of specific street address.
- **Payment Standards** are set by each local Public Housing Authority (PHA) and may range from **90% to 110% of the FMR**. The actual amount paid may differ slightly from the FMR listed above.
- **Small Area FMRs (SAFMRs)** apply in certain metro areas and are set at the zip code level, which may result in different amounts than shown above. Check with the local PHA for zip-code-specific rates.
- FMRs shown are **FY 2026** values, effective August 1, 2025.

**Data Source:** [HUD FY 2026 Fair Market Rents](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/fmr.html)

## 2. Target Audience

Understanding who is most likely to rent at River Garden Estates is the foundation of an effective campaign. Three primary audience segments have been identified:

**Segment A — Families with Children.** The 2–5 bedroom floor plans, playground, basketball court, and spacious patios make this property ideal for families. Parents aged 25–45 in the Sacramento metro area who are searching for more space than a standard apartment can offer represent the highest-conversion audience.

**Segment B — Section 8 / Housing Voucher Holders.** Sacramento County has a significant population of households holding Housing Choice Vouchers. Explicitly advertising voucher acceptance in all ad copy removes a major barrier to inquiry and attracts a motivated, pre-qualified renter pool.

**Segment C — Relocating Households.** Sacramento continues to attract residents from the Bay Area and other high-cost California metros seeking more affordable space. Households relocating for work, family, or cost-of-living reasons actively search for larger units and respond well to value-focused messaging.

## 3. Facebook & Instagram Advertising Strategy

### 3.1 Campaign Structure

The paid social campaign should be structured as two separate campaigns within Facebook Ads Manager, each with distinct objectives and ad sets.

| Campaign | Objective | Ad Sets | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Campaign 1: Awareness** | Reach / Brand Awareness | 1 — Sacramento Metro (25-mile radius) | $15/day |
| **Campaign 2: Lead Generation** | Lead Generation / Messages | 2 — Families; 3 — Voucher Holders | $20/day |

**Recommended Total Daily Budget:** $35/day (~$1,050/month)
**Minimum Viable Budget:** $25/day (~$750/month)

### 3.2 Targeting Parameters

**Geographic:** Sacramento, CA — 15-mile radius from the property address. Include zip codes: 95823, 95824, 95828, 95832, 95838, 95841.

**Demographics:**
- Age: 25–55
- Household size: 3+ (for family targeting)
- Income: $30,000–$80,000/year

**Interests & Behaviors:**
- Apartment/home searching
- Moving & relocation
- Family & parenting
- Sacramento community groups

**Custom Audiences:**
- Retargeting: Website visitors (if applicable) and anyone who engaged with previous posts
- Lookalike Audience: Build from existing tenant contact list (1–2% lookalike)

**Special Ad Category:** Because this is a housing advertisement, it **must** be set to the **"Housing" Special Ad Category** in Facebook Ads Manager. This disables age, gender, and zip code targeting but enables the housing-compliant audience tool. This is a legal requirement under the Fair Housing Act.

### 5.3 Community & Referral Outreach

**Resident Referral Program:** Offer current residents a $100–$200 rent credit for referring a new tenant who signs a lease. Word-of-mouth from satisfied residents is the highest-trust form of advertising.

**Local Community Boards:** Post flyers at:
- Sacramento Housing Authority offices
- Local churches and community centers
- Laundromats and grocery stores in the 95823/95824 zip codes
- Sacramento Employment & Training Agency (SETA) offices

**Section 8 Outreach:** Contact the Sacramento Housing and Redevelopment Agency (SHRA) directly to be listed as a participating landlord. SHRA maintains a landlord directory that voucher holders actively use.

## 9. Fair Housing Compliance

All advertising must comply with the **Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. § 3604)**. Key requirements:

- Do **not** include language that indicates preference for or against any protected class (race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability)
- Use the Equal Housing Opportunity logo or statement on all print materials
- Facebook/Instagram housing ads **must** use the "Housing" Special Ad Category
- Avoid phrases like "perfect for couples," "quiet adult community," or "ideal for professionals" — these may imply discrimination against families or other protected classes
- The phrase "Section 8 Welcome" is affirmative and encouraged in California, where source-of-income discrimination is prohibited under FEHA

# River Garden Estates — Vacancy Marketing Plan
**Sacramento, CA | Prepared: May 2026**

---

## Executive Summary

This marketing plan outlines a comprehensive strategy to fill vacancies at River Garden Estates in Sacramento, CA. The property offers spacious 2–5 bedroom townhome-style apartments with family-friendly amenities and accepts Section 8 vouchers. The strategy focuses on targeted Facebook and Instagram advertising, free listing syndication, and community outreach to reach families and voucher holders seeking affordable, spacious living.

## 2. Target Audience

**Segment A — Families with Children**
Families in the Sacramento area looking for more space than a traditional apartment. The 2–5 bedroom layouts, private enclosed patios, and on-site playgrounds make this property highly attractive to households with children who need room to grow.

**Segment B — Section 8 / Voucher Holders**
Since the property welcomes Section 8 vouchers, this is a critical demographic. Many voucher holders struggle to find properties that accept their vouchers, especially for larger unit sizes (3+ bedrooms). Highlighting this acceptance will drive significant inquiry volume.

**Segment C — Relocating Households seeking Value**
Renters moving within or to Sacramento who prioritize space and affordability over luxury downtown amenities. The inclusion of water and trash in the rent adds to the value proposition.

## 9. Fair Housing Compliance

- Use **"Housing" Special Ad Category** in Facebook Ads Manager (required)
- No language implying preference for/against any protected class
- Include Equal Housing Opportunity logo on print materials
- In California: "Section 8 Welcome" is affirmative and encouraged (FEHA prohibits source-of-income discrimination)
- Avoid: "perfect for couples," "quiet adult community," "ideal for professionals"

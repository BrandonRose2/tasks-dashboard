**Prepared by:** Manus AI

**Property:** River Garden Estates, 2201 Northview Dr, Sacramento, CA 95833

## Executive Summary

River Garden Estates offers a unique value proposition in the competitive Sacramento rental market. With spacious 2- to 5-bedroom townhome-style units ranging from 1,050 to 1,478 square feet, the property is ideally suited for families and individuals seeking more space than a traditional apartment. The property's acceptance of Section 8 vouchers and its family-friendly amenities (playgrounds, picnic areas, basketball court) make it a highly desirable community for its target demographic. 

This marketing plan outlines a comprehensive strategy to fill current vacancies by leveraging targeted digital advertising on Facebook and Instagram, optimizing online listings, and engaging with the local community.

## Market Context

The Sacramento rental market remains competitive, with average rents hovering around $1,850 to $1,900 per month across all unit types [1] [2]. However, larger units (3+ bedrooms) are in high demand, especially those offering family-friendly amenities and affordable options. River Garden Estates, with rents starting at $2,400 for 2-bedroom units and up to $4,579 for 5-bedroom townhomes, is positioned to attract families needing substantial living space. The property's location near Woodlake Elementary School and Grant High School further enhances its appeal to families [3].

## Target Audience

The primary target audience for River Garden Estates includes:

1.  **Families:** The 2- to 5-bedroom layouts, private enclosed patios, and on-site playgrounds make this an ideal community for families with children.
2.  **Section 8 Voucher Holders:** The property explicitly accepts Section 8 vouchers, which is a significant selling point that should be prominently featured in all marketing materials.
3.  **Local Renters Seeking Space:** Individuals or multi-generational households looking for townhome-style living rather than traditional apartment layouts.

## Digital Advertising Strategy: Facebook & Instagram

Social media advertising is the most effective way to reach the target demographic in Sacramento. The campaign will utilize both Facebook and Instagram to maximize reach and engagement.

### Campaign Objectives

*   **Primary:** Generate qualified leads (phone calls and tour bookings) for vacant units.
*   **Secondary:** Increase brand awareness of River Garden Estates within the Sacramento area.

### Targeting Parameters

*   **Location:** Sacramento, CA (+ 15-mile radius).
*   **Demographics:** Ages 25-55.
*   **Interests:** Real estate, apartments, family, parenting, affordable housing.
*   **Behaviors:** Likely to move, currently renting.

## Additional Marketing Initiatives

To maximize the impact of the digital ads, the following supplementary strategies should be implemented:

### 1. Optimize Online Listings

Ensure that River Garden Estates is prominently and accurately listed on major rental platforms, including Apartments.com, Zillow, and AffordableHousing.com. 

*   **Action:** Update all listings with high-quality photos, accurate pricing, and a comprehensive list of amenities. Highlight the acceptance of Section 8 vouchers in the listing titles or primary descriptions.

### 2. Community Outreach

Engage with local community organizations and housing authorities to generate referrals.

*   **Action:** Distribute flyers and property information to the Sacramento Housing and Redevelopment Agency (SHRA) and local community centers like The GreenHouse.

### 3. Referral Program

Incentivize current residents to refer new tenants.

*   **Action:** Implement a resident referral program offering a rent credit (e.g., $200) for every successful referral that signs a lease.

## Budget Allocation (Recommended)

*   **Facebook/Instagram Ads:** $500 - $800 (Approx. $15-$25/day)

## Measurement and Optimization

The success of the campaign will be measured by tracking the following key performance indicators (KPIs):

*   Number of leads generated (calls, emails, form submissions).
*   Cost per lead (CPL).
*   Number of scheduled tours.
*   Number of signed leases.

The Facebook and Instagram ads should be monitored weekly. If certain ad variations or targeting parameters are underperforming, the budget should be reallocated to the most effective segments.

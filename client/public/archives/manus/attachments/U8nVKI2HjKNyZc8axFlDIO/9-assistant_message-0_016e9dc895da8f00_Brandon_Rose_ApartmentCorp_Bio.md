### BIOGRAPHY
**Driving Operational Excellence Through Technology**

Brandon Rose brings a unique blend of executive management, technical engineering, and advanced AI expertise to ApartmentCorp. With a strong foundation in both business operations and emerging technologies, Brandon plays a pivotal role in driving strategic initiatives and special projects that enhance the company's technological capabilities and operational efficiency.

Before joining ApartmentCorp, Brandon spent seven years in executive management, directing operations for a multi-million-dollar service and equipment company. In this role, he successfully oversaw a 47-employee organization, managing everything from payroll and compliance to business development. His leadership in implementing electronic invoicing systems and wireless-enabled field reporting demonstrated his early commitment to leveraging technology to streamline complex logistical operations.

Brandon’s transition into real estate advisory further honed his skills in market analysis, property valuations, and complex negotiations. Representing buyers, sellers, and investors in both residential and commercial transactions across Los Angeles, he developed a deep understanding of the real estate market and the critical importance of data-driven decision-making.

At ApartmentCorp, Brandon leverages his highly analytical mindset—rooted in his Bachelor of Science in Petroleum Engineering from Texas Tech University—to support the company's technology-driven operational excellence. He is highly adept at integrating artificial intelligence platforms, such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, alongside advanced productivity tools like Notion and Slack. By applying these emerging technologies to workflow automation, data analysis, and strategic content creation, Brandon helps ensure that ApartmentCorp and its groundbreaking platform, Property MAX AI, remain at the forefront of innovation in the multifamily housing industry.

"Leveraging advanced technology and data-driven insights to streamline operations and build smarter, more efficient real estate systems."

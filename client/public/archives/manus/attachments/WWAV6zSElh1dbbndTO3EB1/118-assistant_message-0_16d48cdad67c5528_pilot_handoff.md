## Property data included

The internal pilot matrix includes property codes and names, Region 3 designation, verified property-manager names and business mailboxes, supporting contacts, shared-manager relationships, missing role identities, combined-property naming issues, and confirmed business contact fields when directly available. For Marrero 3, the source also verifies the property address, office phone and extension, mobile number, and CST time zone.[1]

Known items now represented as accountable work include Jennifer Frederick's P01/P04 dual-property coverage, Sandra Crump's P02/P06 coverage, the P03/P07 manager-email overlap, missing P07 manager and assistant names, the missing P09 reception identity, P10's leasing-versus-manager mailbox question, and the combined P11 Yorkshire/Windsor Village display and routing rule.

> **Recommended test:** Discuss one task briefly in the appropriate existing Chat channel, paste its ClickUp link, identify an internal owner, move it through `To do` → `In progress` → `Complete`, and record the final decision in the task. Chat should coordinate the work; the task should remain the accountable record.

All 11 tasks were verified in the `PropertyMax Pilot Tasks` List after creation or enrichment.[2] The public `Operations` and `Managers` channels remain available for internal testing. No Chat messages were posted, no property-team users were invited, and no upper-management assignments were added.

Detailed per-property status workflows, automations, Forms, dashboard refinement, invitations, and resident-level data remain deferred. The expanded [property matrix](expanded_property_matrix.md) is the controlled reference for future enrichment; unavailable values remain blank and are never estimated.

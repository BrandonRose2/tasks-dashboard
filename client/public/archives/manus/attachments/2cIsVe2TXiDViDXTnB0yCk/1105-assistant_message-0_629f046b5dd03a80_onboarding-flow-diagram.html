Regional Manager reviews the hire for alignment with regional staffing needs, confirms the property assignment, and verifies the position title and compensation are within regional guidelines.

⚠️ Rejection at any stage — If any approver rejects a form or the hire:

❌ Rejected → New hire is notified with specific reason · Form returned for correction · Process restarts from that stage

When all credentials are entered, Ethan clicks the blue “Save & Send ‘Let’s Get to Work’ Email” button at the bottom of the IT Provisioning form. Idempotent — email only sends once per hire (provisioningEmailSentAt timestamp prevents duplicates).

Testing Mode currently routes to Brandon only. Bookmarks file is uploaded to S3 and linked directly in the email.

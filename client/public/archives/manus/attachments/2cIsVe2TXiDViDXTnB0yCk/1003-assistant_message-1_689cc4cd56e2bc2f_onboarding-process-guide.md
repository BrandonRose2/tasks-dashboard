# ApartmentCorp New Hire Onboarding Process
## Detailed Step-by-Step Explanation

**Document Version:** May 2026
**Maintained by:** Brandon Rose, HR Administration
**System:** ApartmentCorp New Hire Onboarding Portal

---

## Overview

The ApartmentCorp new hire onboarding process is a structured, multi-phase workflow that takes a candidate from initial registration through their first day on the job. The process is managed through the ApartmentCorp New Hire Onboarding Portal, which coordinates tasks across HR, IT, Accounting, Management, and the new hire themselves. The entire process is designed to be completed within **3 hours** — an automated alert is sent to Brandon if any onboarding session exceeds this threshold.

The approval chain for all new hires follows this sequence:

> **Brandon (HR) → Regional Manager → Robert (Property Manager) → Ethan Cowles (IT) → Nicole (COO) → Marc (CEO)**

---

## Phase 1 — Candidate Intake & Registration

### Step 1.1 — HR Creates the New Hire Record

Brandon or HR administration initiates the onboarding process by registering the new hire in the portal. This involves entering the new hire's email address and assigning them a position and property/location from the available dropdown menus. The system generates a unique 4-digit PIN for the new hire at this stage, which will later serve as the password for their credentials PDF.

### Step 1.2 — Welcome Email Sent to New Hire

Immediately upon registration, the system automatically sends a welcome email to the new hire's email address. This email contains a direct link to the ApartmentCorp Employee Onboarding Portal, their temporary access credentials, and instructions for completing the onboarding process. The email is sent from the ApartmentCorp HR system and is CC'd to Brandon.

### Step 1.3 — New Hire Registers on the Portal

The new hire navigates to the Employee Onboarding Portal using the link in their welcome email. They create their account by entering their email address and the 4-digit PIN provided by HR. This PIN is important — it will be used as the password to open their credentials PDF later in the process.

### Step 1.4 — First Login Notification to Ethan

When the new hire logs into the portal for the first time, the system automatically sends a notification email to **Ethan Cowles (IT)**, CC'd to **Brandon**, alerting them that the new hire has begun the onboarding process and that IT credential provisioning will be needed once the forms are approved.

---

## Phase 2 — Application Forms

The new hire is presented with a series of required forms that must be completed in full before the approval process can begin. All form fields follow ApartmentCorp's visual standards: required fields are outlined in **neon orange**, and upon completion each field turns **neon green** with a checkmark indicator.

### Step 2.1 — Employment Application

The new hire completes a comprehensive employment application capturing their full legal name, contact information, address, position applied for, start date, emergency contact details, and employment history. This form is the foundation of their HR record and is used to populate their name throughout the system.

### Step 2.2 — Federal W-4 (Tax Withholding)

The new hire completes the IRS Federal W-4 form, declaring their filing status, number of dependents, and any additional withholding preferences. This information is passed to Accounting for Paychex payroll setup.

### Step 2.3 — NY IT-2104 (State Tax Withholding)

For New York-based employees, the new hire completes the NY IT-2104 state withholding certificate, which determines how much New York State and City income tax is withheld from each paycheck.

### Step 2.4 — Direct Deposit Authorization

The new hire provides their bank account information (routing number and account number) to authorize direct deposit of their paychecks through Paychex. This form is securely stored and passed to Accounting.

### Step 2.5 — I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification

The new hire completes Section 1 of the federal I-9 form, attesting to their identity and authorization to work in the United States. They must also provide documentation (e.g., passport, driver's license + Social Security card) which is reviewed and verified by HR.

### Step 2.6 — Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure Agreement

The new hire reads and electronically signs ApartmentCorp's confidentiality agreement, acknowledging their obligation to protect sensitive company, resident, and property information.

### Step 2.7 — GPS / Location Tracking Acknowledgment

The new hire acknowledges ApartmentCorp's policy regarding location tracking and GPS monitoring on company devices and during work hours.

### Step 2.8 — Employee Policies Acknowledgment

The new hire reviews and signs the ApartmentCorp Employee Policies document, confirming they have read and understood the company's code of conduct, attendance policies, communication standards, and property management procedures.

### Step 2.9 — Maintenance Skills Assessment (if applicable)

For maintenance-track positions, the new hire completes a skills assessment test covering basic maintenance knowledge, safety procedures, and property upkeep standards. Results are reviewed by the Regional Manager.

---

## Phase 3 — Approval Chain

Once all forms have been submitted, the approval workflow is triggered automatically. Each approver receives an email notification when it is their turn to review. The chain is **sequential** — each person must approve before the next is notified.

### Step 3.1 — Brandon (HR) Reviews & Approves

Brandon reviews all submitted forms for completeness and accuracy. He verifies that the employment application, tax forms, and agreements are properly filled out. He can approve individual forms or reject them with notes requesting corrections. Once satisfied, he approves the submission and the workflow advances to the Regional Manager.

### Step 3.2 — Regional Manager Reviews & Approves

The Regional Manager for the new hire's assigned property reviews the submission. They confirm the position, location assignment, and start date are correct, and verify the maintenance assessment results if applicable. Upon approval, the workflow advances to Robert.

### Step 3.3 — Robert (Property Manager) Reviews & Approves

Robert reviews the new hire's application from a property operations perspective, confirming the hire aligns with property staffing needs and that all required documentation is in order. Upon approval, the workflow advances to Ethan.

### Step 3.4 — Ethan Cowles (IT) Reviews & Approves

Ethan reviews the submission to confirm he has the information needed to provision the new hire's IT credentials. He approves the forms and then proceeds to Phase 4 (IT Credential Provisioning). Upon approval, the workflow advances to Nicole.

### Step 3.5 — Nicole (COO) Reviews & Approves

Nicole reviews the hire from an executive operations standpoint, confirming the position and compensation align with company strategy and budget. Upon approval, the workflow advances to Marc.

### Step 3.6 — Marc (CEO) Final Approval

Marc provides final executive sign-off on the new hire. This is the last step in the approval chain. Once Marc approves, the system marks the approval process as complete and triggers the IT provisioning and training phases.

---

## Phase 4 — IT Credential Provisioning (Ethan Cowles)

### Step 4.1 — Ethan Opens the New Hire's Provisioning Card

In the HR Admin Portal under the **Technology Onboarding** tab, Ethan sees a card for every registered new hire. Each card displays the new hire's name, email, assigned building, form approval status, and a **progress bar** showing the percentage of required platforms that have been provisioned.

### Step 4.2 — Ethan Enters Credentials for Each Platform

Ethan clicks on the new hire's card to open the full credential entry form. For each of the following platforms, Ethan enters the new hire's username, password, and any relevant notes:

| Platform | Category |
|---|---|
| PropertyMAX.ai | Property Management |
| AppWorks | Property Management |
| OneSite | Property Management |
| Yardi | Property Management |
| Connecteam | Operations & Scheduling |
| MyLoneWorkers.com | Inspections Management |
| Paychex | HR & Payroll (entered by Accounting) |
| Phone Portal | Communication |
| ConnectUC | Communication & Collaboration |
| SamePage | Communication & Collaboration |
| VMware Horizon | Remote Desktop |
| Additional Systems | As applicable |

Ethan marks each platform as "Required for this hire" as appropriate. The progress bar on the new hire's card updates in real time as credentials are saved.

### Step 4.3 — Credentials Saved to Database

When Ethan saves the credentials, they are stored securely in the database and immediately become visible to the new hire in their **Company Websites & Logins** tab on the Employee Portal. Each platform card in the new hire's portal shows a green "Your Credentials" section with the username and masked password (with show/hide/copy controls).

### Step 4.4 — Password-Protected Credentials PDF Generated & Emailed

Upon saving credentials, the system automatically generates a **password-protected PDF** containing all of the new hire's login credentials for every provisioned platform. The PDF is password-protected using the new hire's **4-digit PIN** (the same PIN they used to create their portal account). This PDF is emailed directly to the new hire and CC'd to Brandon for records.

### Step 4.5 — Provisioning Complete Notification

Once all required platforms are provisioned (progress bar reaches 100%), Ethan marks the provisioning as complete. A notification is sent to Brandon confirming IT setup is finished and the new hire is ready to proceed.

---

## Phase 5 — Payroll & Accounting Setup (Accounting Team)

### Step 5.1 — Accounting Creates New Hire Profile in Paychex

The Accounting team uses the W-4, IT-2104, and Direct Deposit Authorization forms submitted by the new hire to create their payroll profile in Paychex. This includes assigning the correct pay schedule, department code, pay rate, and tax withholding settings.

### Step 5.2 — Direct Deposit Configured

Accounting enters the new hire's bank account information into Paychex to activate direct deposit. The new hire will receive their first paycheck via direct deposit on the next scheduled pay date.

### Step 5.3 — New Hire Receives Paychex Welcome Email

Paychex automatically sends the new hire a welcome email with a link to set up their employee self-service account. Through this account, the new hire can view pay stubs, request PTO, update their tax withholding, and manage benefits enrollment.

### Step 5.4 — Benefits Enrollment Window Opened

Accounting opens the benefits enrollment window in Paychex. The new hire has a defined window (typically 30 days from hire date) to select health insurance, dental, vision, and any other available benefits. Accounting confirms enrollment is complete before closing the window.

---

## Phase 6 — Training Videos

### Step 6.1 — New Hire Accesses Training Library

The new hire navigates to the **Training Videos** tab in their Employee Portal. This section contains a curated library of platform training videos organized by system.

### Step 6.2 — Platform Training Videos

The new hire watches training videos for each of the platforms they will use in their role:

- **PropertyMAX.ai** — property management workflows, resident records, and reporting
- **OneSite** — leasing, renewals, and resident communications
- **Connecteam** — scheduling, time tracking, and team communications
- **MyLoneWorkers.com** — creating, scheduling, and completing property inspection reports
- **Yardi** — accounting, maintenance work orders, and property financials
- **ConnectUC** — unified communications and phone system usage
- **SamePage** — team collaboration, project tracking, and document sharing

### Step 6.3 — Training Completion Acknowledged

After completing the training videos, the new hire acknowledges completion in the portal. This acknowledgment is recorded in the Document Hub under "Software Training Completion."

---

## Phase 7 — Document Hub & Records Review

### Step 7.1 — HR Reviews All Submitted Documents

Brandon accesses the **Document Hub** in the HR Admin Dashboard and selects the new hire from the dropdown. All submitted forms automatically appear in their corresponding category cards with color-coded status badges (Submitted, Approved, or Rejected).

### Step 7.2 — Individual Form Review

Brandon can open and review each submitted form individually. For forms requiring correction, he can reject them with a note, which triggers a notification to the new hire to resubmit.

### Step 7.3 — Bulk Approve / Reject

For efficiency, Brandon can use the **Bulk Approve** or **Bulk Reject** action bar to process all pending forms for a new hire at once. A confirmation dialog prevents accidental bulk actions.

### Step 7.4 — Document Status Badges

Each new hire in the Document Hub dropdown displays a visual status badge:

- **All Approved** — all required forms have been reviewed and approved
- **Pending** — some forms are still awaiting review
- **Action Required** — one or more forms have been rejected and need resubmission

---

## Phase 8 — Day 1 Onsite Orientation

### Step 8.1 — Regional Manager Conducts Property Tour

On the new hire's first day, the Regional Manager meets them at the property and conducts a full orientation tour. This includes introducing the new hire to the existing team, showing them the property layout, identifying maintenance areas, and reviewing emergency procedures and safety protocols.

### Step 8.2 — Keys, Access Cards & Equipment Issued

The Regional Manager issues the new hire all physical access items required for their role: property keys, access cards or fobs, any company-issued devices, and uniforms or PPE as applicable.

### Step 8.3 — New Hire Accesses All Provisioned Systems

The new hire logs into each of their provisioned platforms for the first time using the credentials provided by Ethan. The Regional Manager confirms that all logins are working and assists with any access issues.

### Step 8.4 — Regional Manager Completes Orientation Checklist in HR Portal

The Regional Manager logs into the HR Portal and marks the Day 1 orientation checklist as complete, confirming the new hire has been introduced to the team, shown the property, and has working access to all required systems.

---

## Phase 9 — Onboarding Complete

### Step 9.1 — Final Sign-Off by Brandon & Marc

Brandon reviews the completed onboarding record and provides final HR sign-off. Marc provides executive confirmation that the new hire is officially onboarded and active.

### Step 9.2 — 3-Hour Onboarding Alert (Automated)

If the onboarding process has been in progress for more than **3 hours** without reaching completion, the system automatically sends an alert email to Brandon. This alert includes the new hire's name, email, how long they have been in the process, and which steps are still incomplete, allowing HR to follow up proactively.

### Step 9.3 — Onboarding Completion Email Sent

Upon completion, the system sends a confirmation email to the new hire welcoming them officially to the ApartmentCorp team. A summary notification is also sent to Brandon and Marc confirming the onboarding is fully complete.

---

## Phase 10 — Server File Archiving

### Step 10.1 — All Files Filed to Service Server

Upon completion of the onboarding process, all submitted forms, signed documents, training acknowledgments, and the credentials PDF are automatically archived to the ApartmentCorp service server in the following path:

> **Source → Server → Core Data → New Hire Onboarding Folders**

Each new hire has their own dedicated folder within this directory, organized by name and hire date. This ensures all onboarding records are preserved for compliance, auditing, and future reference.

### Step 10.2 — Document Hub Records Retained

All records in the Document Hub remain accessible to HR administrators indefinitely. The new hire's profile, submitted forms, approval history, and credential provisioning record are retained in the portal database for ongoing reference.

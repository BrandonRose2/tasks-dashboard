## SECTION 1 — COMP ELIGIBILITY RULES

Before accepting any comp, confirm it meets ALL of the following criteria:

- [ ] Property is a **conventional market-rate** multifamily community (not income-restricted, not HUD/Section 8 only, not senior-only 55+, not manufactured/mobile homes)
- [ ] Property is an **apartment community** (not a single-family rental, duplex, or small landlord unit)
- [ ] Property was **built within approximately ±12 years** of the subject property's year of construction
- [ ] Property is **not a luxury/Class A** community if the subject property is Class B or C
- [ ] Property is **located within a reasonable geographic radius** of the subject (same city preferred; expand to nearest metro if local supply is insufficient)
- [ ] Rent data is **current** (pulled within the last 30 days)
- [ ] Rent shown is **base rent** (note separately if fees or utilities are included in the advertised price)

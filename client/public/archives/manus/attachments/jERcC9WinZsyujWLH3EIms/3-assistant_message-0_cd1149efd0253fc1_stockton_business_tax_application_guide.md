# City of Stockton Business License Tax Application Guide

Based on the draft provided by your COO and the requirements outlined by the City of Stockton, I have compiled the necessary information to help you complete the Business License Tax Application for **Granite Ridge Housing LP**.

## Application Overview and Requirements

The City of Stockton requires any person or entity engaged in the business of renting or letting rooms, apartments, or other accommodations with three or more units to obtain a Business License Tax Certificate [1]. The application process involves completing the Business License Tax Application, providing information about the business entity, and calculating the required taxes and fees based on gross receipts.

### Tax and Fee Structure

For a rental property business (Standard Industrial Classification 6513), the City of Stockton imposes the following fees and taxes:

1. **Annual Registration Tax**: A flat fee of $24.00 per year [2].
2. **Gross Receipts Tax (Mill Tax)**: The tax rate for commercial rental and apartment operators is **nine-tenths of one mill ($0.0009) for each dollar of gross receipts** [3].
3. **Penalties**: The City of Stockton imposes a penalty of 15% per month on delinquent taxes, up to a maximum of 60%. Additionally, operating without a license incurs an extra 25% penalty [4].

The calculations provided by your COO on page 4 of the draft correctly apply these rates. The gross receipts tax is calculated as `Gross Income × 0.0009`, and the penalties are calculated at 85% (60% maximum monthly penalty + 25% operating without a license penalty).

## Submission Instructions

Once the application is fully completed and signed, it can be submitted to the City of Stockton along with the payment for the total amount due.

*   **In-Person or Mail**: City of Stockton, Administrative Services Department, Revenue Services Division - Business License Tax, 425 North El Dorado Street, PO Box 1570, Stockton, CA 95201 [5].
*   **Email**: Applications can also be emailed to BL@stocktonca.gov [5].
*   **Payment Options**: The City accepts cash, check, money order, debit card, MasterCard, or Visa [1].

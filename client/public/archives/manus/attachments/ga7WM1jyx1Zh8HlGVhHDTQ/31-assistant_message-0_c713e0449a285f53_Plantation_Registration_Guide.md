# City of Plantation Registration Guide for 101 Holly LLC

This guide outlines the step-by-step process and requirements for obtaining both a **Local Business Tax Receipt** and a **Vacation Rental Registration** from the City of Plantation, Florida, for your entity, 101 Holly LLC.

## 1. Local Business Tax Receipt

Pursuant to Chapter 14 of the City of Plantation Code of Ordinances, any business operating within the city must obtain a Local Business Tax Receipt prior to operation [1].

### Application Process

Effective August 24, 2023, all new accounts must be initiated through the City's Citizen Access portal [1].

1. **Prepare the Paper Application**: Download and complete the paper "Application for Local Business Tax Receipt." This form must be 100% complete, including an original wet ink signature and notary [1].
2. **Register Online**: Register as a user on the City of Plantation Citizen Access Portal (https://aca.plantation.org/CitizenAccess/default.aspx) [1].
3. **Create the Record**: 
   - Click on "Start a New Application" and select "Local Business Tax."
   - **Step 1 (Address and Contact)**: Search for the property address. The Parcel Number and Property Owner will automatically populate. Enter the four contacts exactly as completed on the paper application [1].
   - **Step 2 (Application Data)**: Enter the Business Tax information exactly as completed on the paper application [1].
   - **Step 3 (Review)**: Double-check that the data entry matches the paper application and agree to the certification [1].
   - **Step 4 (Pay Fees)**: Pay the required $15 application fee online [1].
   - **Step 5 (Record Issuance & Upload)**: Click on "Upload Plans and Documents." Upload your saved, notarized paper application [1].
4. **City Review**: Monitor your email for correspondence from the City regarding any issues or additional payments required [1].

### Fees

- **Application Fee**: $15 (plus a $1 processing fee if paid by card online) [1].
- **Code Compliance Inspection Fee**: $270 (if required for commercial zoning districts) [1].
- **Annual Local Business Tax Fee**: Varies based on business classification, typically between $105 and $210 [1].

## 2. Vacation Rental Registration

The City of Plantation requires owners to register their vacation rental properties to ensure appropriate management and protect public health and safety [2]. A vacation rental is defined as a dwelling unit rented to guests more than three times in a calendar year for periods of less than 30 days [2].

### Required Documents

For a non-hosted vacation rental owned by an LLC, you must gather the following documents before applying [3]:

1. **Proof of Property Ownership**: Provide proof from the Broward County Property Appraiser’s website. Since the property is owned by 101 Holly LLC, provide ownership information found on Sunbiz [3].
2. **Licenses**:
   - **Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR)** license [3].
   - **Department of Revenue**: If your hosting platform (e.g., Airbnb, VRBO) collects and remits state sales tax, you must acknowledge this on the City’s Affidavit of Compliance and provide proof of the platform's registration [3].
   - **Broward County Tourist Development Tax**: Similar to state tax, if the platform collects this, provide proof of their account with the Broward County Tax Collector [3].
   - **Broward County Business Tax Receipt**: Apply online or in person with Broward County [3].
   - **City of Plantation Business Tax Receipt**: (See Section 1 above) [3].
3. **Certificate of Insurance**: Obtain a commercial certificate of insurance covering liability for injury or harm to occupants. The City of Plantation must be listed as the Certificate holder. Required coverage amounts:
   - Bodily Injury & Property Damage Liability (Each Occurrence): $1,000,000
   - Policy Aggregate: $2,000,000
   - Personal & Advertising Injury: $1,000,000 [3].
4. **Interior Sketch**: A floor plan (can be hand-drawn) labeling each room, bedroom closets, placement of exterior noise detection devices, and an evacuation map [3].
5. **Exterior Sketch**: A current survey or aerial photo identifying property boundaries, structures, pools/spas, fencing, noise detection devices, and off-street parking spaces [3].
6. **Lease Agreement**: A sample copy of the lease agreement furnished to guests, including mandatory information required by the Vacation Rental Ordinance [3].
7. **Affidavit of Compliance**: Complete the applicable affidavit demonstrating knowledge of the responsibility to maintain compliance with all laws [3].

### Application and Inspection Process

1. **Submit Application**: Submit the complete application with all required documents [3].
2. **Pay Fees**: Once the application is reviewed, you will receive an email to pay the Vacation Rental Registration Fee, Safety Inspection Fee, and Business Tax Fee [3].
3. **Safety Inspection**: Upon payment of the registration and business tax fees, the property will be scheduled for a safety inspection [3].

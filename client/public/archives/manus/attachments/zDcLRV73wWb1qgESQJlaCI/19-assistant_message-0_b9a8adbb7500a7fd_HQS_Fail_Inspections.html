Items Most Likely to Fail HQS Inspections
24-Hour Emergency Repair Items
  • Smoke Detectors: Inoperable (including missing or bad batteries), missing or removed smoke detectors.
  • Utilities: Gas, Electric and/or Water not in Service.
  • Major Plumbing Leaks or Flooding.
Non-Emergency Items
  • Deteriorating paint surfaces (peeling, chipping, cracked, water damaged, etc.) or stained, cracked or buckling ceilings (interior or exterior).
  • Loose handrails (interior or exterior).
  • Absence of a handrail where there are four or more consecutive steps (interior or exterior).
  • Absent or non-secured railings around a porch or balcony, which is 30 inches or more above the ground.
  • Messy yards, grass not cut, trash, or other debris left on site or in the interior of the unit.
  • Infestation of roaches, rodents, bed bugs, or other insects.
  • Tuckpointing needed in foundation walls or other opening requiring caulking or sealing.
  • Deficient door weather-stripping allowing unacceptable air infiltration in unit.
  • Broken or cracked windows or torn screens (lower level only).
  • Inoperable window (will not open or fully close).
  • Windows without locks.
  • Loose light fixtures, missing globes, missing or blown light bulbs and non-operative switches.
  • Tripping hazards, such as torn carpet, vinyl, etc.
  • Kitchen: Dirty stove burners or burners do not light on command.
  • Gaps or unsealed openings beneath kitchen sinks or bathroom vanity sinks.
  • Plumbing problems including leaks, broken or dripping faucets and running toilets.
  • Loose toilets.
  • No operable window or air ventilation fan in the bathroom.
  • Loose electrical outlets, broken or missing outlet covers or electric wire connections not enclosed in junction boxes.
  • Gaps in electrical fittings & circuit breaker boxes.
  • Furnace or water heater flues not properly aligned and sealed at chimney.
  • Slope of flue on water heater and furnace to ensure proper drafting and ventilation.
  • Dirty furnace filter.
  • Water heater discharge line leaking, missing, or not terminating approximately 6 inches from the floor.

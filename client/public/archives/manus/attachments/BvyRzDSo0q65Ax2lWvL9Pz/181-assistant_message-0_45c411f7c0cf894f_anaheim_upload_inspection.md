# Anaheim Upload Inspection

The two newly uploaded PDFs are not the underlying 2020 or 2021 federal tax-return packages.

- Both files are three-page **document-index/listing PDFs**.
- The 2020 index lists historical Anaheim tax returns and K-1 documents, rather than including the documents themselves.
- The 2021 index lists the 2021 U.S. Tax Return, individual K-1s, and related California/e-file documents, rather than including the documents themselves.
- Neither upload contains the actual Form 1065 return pages or embedded federal K-1 pages required for the approved redaction workflow.

No document was altered. The required source for each year is the actual complete federal return PDF that appears in the index as the 2020 or 2021 Anaheim U.S. Tax Return, including its embedded federal K-1 packet(s).

## 2020 source observation

The newly uploaded 2020 PDF is a 62-page combined federal and California return package, not a standalone California-only filing. Pages 42 and 43 are federal Form 1065 attachments (Forms 8825 and 8886), and are not investor K-1 pages. The page-structure scan detected a federal Schedule K-1 marker later in the package; the actual federal K-1 packet boundary must therefore be determined from the original sequence before any production proposal is issued.

## 2020 boundary finding

Pages 42–44 are federal Form 1065 attachments (Form 8825 and a two-page Form 8886). Page 45 begins California Form 565 material, confirming a federal-to-state transition after page 44. No embedded federal Schedule K-1 packet was found in the retained federal pages. The prior text marker was a false positive from a federal attachment reference to Schedule K-1, rather than an actual K-1 form. Under the approved workflow, no redacted production should be created from this source until the complete federal K-1 attachment packet is supplied or its source location is confirmed.

## 2021 boundary finding

The uploaded 2021 PDF is a 67-page combined federal and California package. Page 49 begins California Form 565 material, establishing the federal-to-state transition after page 48. The apparent Schedule K-1 text marker on page 45 was a false positive within a federal Form 8825 reference, not an actual Schedule K-1. No embedded federal K-1 packet was found in the uploaded federal pages. As with 2020, a compliant tax-return production cannot be created from this source until the complete federal K-1 attachment packet is supplied or its source location is confirmed.

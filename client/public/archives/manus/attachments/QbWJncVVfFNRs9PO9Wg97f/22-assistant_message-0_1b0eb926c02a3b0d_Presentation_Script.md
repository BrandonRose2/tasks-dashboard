# Foxen Invoice Analysis: Presentation Script

**Title Slide: Foxen Administration Charges Breakdown (Jan 2025 – Mar 2026)**

**Speaker Notes:**
"Good morning, everyone. Today, I'll be walking you through our analysis of the Foxen Administration invoices. We've compiled and reviewed 15 months of billing data—from January 2025 through March 2026—to break down our charges per property, per month. This review gives us a clear picture of our total spend, where our costs are concentrated, and how our portfolio expansion has impacted our monthly run rate."

---

**Slide 1: Overall Spend & Top Cost Drivers**

**Visual Suggestion:** A pie chart showing the top 3 properties vs. the rest of the portfolio, alongside the total spend figure.

**Speaker Notes:**
"Let's start with the big picture. Over this 15-month period, our total spend with Foxen was $32,553.72. 

What stands out immediately is that our costs are highly concentrated. Just three properties account for over 56% of our total invoice costs. Gates On Manhattan Apartments is our largest driver by far, representing exactly 30% of the total spend at over $9,700. This is followed by River Pointe Apartments at 15.3%, and Colonial Estates in Thibodaux at 11.6%. 

Understanding this concentration is key, as any rate negotiations or unit adjustments at these three properties will have an outsized impact on our bottom line."

---

**Slide 2: Portfolio Expansion Impact (The September Jump)**

**Visual Suggestion:** A bar chart or line graph showing monthly totals from Jan 2025 to Mar 2026, highlighting the sharp increase in September 2025.

**Speaker Notes:**
"Moving to the timeline of our expenses, we observed a major shift in the second half of 2025. 

From January through August of 2025, our monthly invoice was very stable, averaging around $1,480 and covering just 4 to 5 properties. However, in September 2025, we saw a significant expansion. Nine new properties were added to the billing cycle—including Arbor Crest, Bayou Pointe, and Marrero 3, among others. 

This portfolio addition caused our monthly total to essentially double overnight, jumping by nearly $1,500 in a single month to reach just over $3,000. Since then, our monthly costs have remained relatively steady at this new, higher baseline."

---

**Slide 3: Year-over-Year Run Rate Comparison**

**Visual Suggestion:** Two side-by-side comparison boxes showing the 2025 monthly average vs. the 2026 monthly average, with a bold "45.8% Increase" callout.

**Speaker Notes:**
"Because of that September expansion, looking at our year-over-year averages tells an important story for our current budget. 

In 2025, our average monthly cost was roughly $1,988. But as we look at our run rate for the first quarter of 2026, we are now averaging $2,898 per month. 

This represents a 45.8% increase in our average monthly cost heading into this year. It's important to note that this isn't due to rate hikes, but rather the volume of new properties now utilizing the Foxen Waiver program."

---

**Slide 4: Summary & Next Steps**

**Visual Suggestion:** Bullet points summarizing the key takeaways: Total Spend, Concentration, Expansion Impact, and Next Steps.

**Speaker Notes:**
"To summarize: we have a solid grasp on our $32,500 spend, we know that Gates On Manhattan is our primary cost driver, and we've accurately tracked the budget impact of our September 2025 property additions. 

I've provided the full Excel breakdown to the team, which details every charge down to the penny. 

As a next step, I recommend we review the unit counts at our top three properties to ensure the billed quantities align with our current occupancy and waiver enrollment. 

Thank you, and I'm happy to answer any questions or dive deeper into specific property numbers."

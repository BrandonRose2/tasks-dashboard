### 2. Employee Training Program — Built from Scratch

I designed and built a complete employee training infrastructure for Apartment Corp's property managers across all regions.

**Phone Excellence Program:** A full training module built around the "5 Golden Questions" for converting prospects into tenants. I produced professional AI avatar training videos using HeyGen, built interactive quizzes, and configured the admin portal with CSV-based scoring for all property managers. This program was deployed and presented via Zoom to managers across multiple properties.

**PropertyMax.ai Training Hub:** A multi-section video training portal covering the full PropertyMax.ai platform — Dashboard, CRM/Directory, Collections, To-Do's, Appointments, Text Messages, Section 8 Inspections, Maintenance Requests, and more. 

### 3. Data Management & Automation Systems

**Automated Financial Reporting:** Monthly Net Cash Flow reports are automatically generated and delivered on the 12th of every month. Reports include region breakdowns, pre-leased vs. unleased comparisons, and at-risk property flags.

**Foxen Integration:** Built and maintained the Foxen Property List, Foxen Resident List, and Foxen Renters Insurance Policy databases — tracking enrollment and compliance across all properties.

**Comparable Market Analysis Automation:** Integrated live data from Co-Star, Apartments.com, and Zillow to track Fair Market Rents (FMR) against current property performance, filtered by zip code and property type.

### 4. Marketing Infrastructure

I built an end-to-end marketing workflow for rental vacancies: downloading property photos from authenticated portals (SharePoint), generating production-ready Facebook and Instagram ad creatives (three formats per property), and producing comprehensive marketing plans with Fair Housing-compliant ad copy.

## 2. Key Systems & Infrastructure Built

Brandon has moved Apartment Corp away from manual tracking and into automated, scalable software solutions.

### A. Custom Software Applications Deployed
- **PropertyMax.ai (SaaS Platform):** Built and launched the MVP and V2.0 Operations Hub. This includes a full CRM directory (Prospects, Tenants, Vendors), maintenance ticket tracking, and a dashboard for property managers.
- **Vacancy Report WebApp:** Developed a dedicated application to track daily vacancy changes, including a scrolling news-ticker UI for real-time updates, and automated alerts for "At-Risk Properties" (vacancy > 4%).
- **Apartment Corp Org Chart Website:** Built and deployed a live React/Tailwind website (`aptorgchart-8jqkkzne.manus.space`) mapping all 43+ properties across 5 regions, complete with automated data extraction from Excel.

### B. Training & Onboarding Infrastructure
- **PropertyMax.ai Training Hub:** Created a comprehensive video training portal for staff.
- **Phone Excellence Program:** Developed a complete training module focusing on the "5 Golden Questions" for prospect conversion. Utilized HeyGen AI to create professional avatar training videos and built an admin portal to track employee quiz scores.

### C. Marketing & Revenue Operations
- **Automated Marketing Workflows:** Built systems to automatically extract property photos from authenticated portals (SharePoint) and generate production-ready Facebook and Instagram ad creatives.
- **Financial Reporting:** Automated the Monthly Net Cash Flow reports (delivered on the 12th of every month) and integrated live comparable data (Co-Star, Zillow) to track Fair Market Rents (FMR) against current property performance.
- **Foxen Integration:** Managed the Foxen Resident List and Renters Insurance Policy databases, ensuring compliance and accurate tracking across regions.

## 3. Business Impact & ROI

The systems Brandon has built provide immediate, measurable value to Apartment Corp:
1. **Increased Conversion Rates:** The Phone Excellence training and CRM tracking directly target prospect-to-tenant conversion, gamifying the process with KPI tracking and rewards.
2. **Operational Efficiency:** Automating the Vacancy Reports and Net Cash Flow data saves dozens of hours of manual clerical work per week, providing upper management with real-time, accurate data.
3. **Professionalized Marketing:** The automated ad generation ensures vacancies are marketed faster and more professionally, reducing days-on-market for empty units.
4. **Scalable Infrastructure:** The Notion Workstation and custom web apps provide a foundation that can easily scale as Apartment Corp acquires new properties (e.g., the recent addition of Crossroads of Lee Summit).

## Conclusion
The scope of work Brandon is currently executing aligns with a **Director of Operations, Systems Architect, or Technical Project Manager**, roles that typically command a salary significantly higher than the $30/hour assistant rate. The infrastructure built has fundamentally modernized how Apartment Corp tracks vacancies, trains employees, and manages property data.

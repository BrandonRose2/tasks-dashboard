Subject: Test3 - Important Hurricane Season Preparedness Notice & Resources

Dear Residents,

As we enter the official hurricane season in Florida, meteorologists are predicting a season with potentially high-intensity storms. Now is the time to prepare yourself, your family, and your household for the possibility of severe weather, tropical storms, hurricanes, flooding, power outages, and emergency evacuations.

Attached to this email, you will find important hurricane preparedness information, including:
- A Hurricane Season Preparedness Notice from the Boca Ciega Management Team
- Recommendations from the Florida Dept. of Health for "Before the Storm Safety" and "After the Storm Safety"
- A Disaster Supply Checklist to help you build your emergency kit
- A FEMA Guide to Documenting and Insuring Your Property

**Key Reminders:**
- **Make a Plan:** Use resources like the Florida Department of Emergency Management's online tool at Ready.gov to create a customized plan for your family.
- **Emergency Supplies:** Maintain a well-stocked emergency preparedness kit to last you and your family for a minimum of seven days, including drinking water, non-perishable food, medications, flashlights, and portable phone chargers.
- **Secure Loose Items:** Before a storm approaches, please secure all outdoor furniture, decorations, grills, planters, and any other loose items that could become airborne and cause property damage or injury.
- **Evacuation Orders:** If an evacuation order is issued, please follow all instructions provided by local emergency management officials. Do not remain in your apartment if authorities direct residents to evacuate.

Please review the attached documents carefully to aid in your plan preparation. Taking the steps to get prepared now will help reduce stress later when access to supplies may be limited due to demand as storms approach.

Please note that during a declared State of Emergency, management and maintenance personnel are also required to follow local emergency orders and seek shelter. As a result, staff may not be available to respond to maintenance requests or emergency calls while emergency conditions exist. Following a storm, our staff will assess the property as soon as conditions are safe and local authorities permit travel. 

Thank you for your cooperation and commitment to keeping our community safe during hurricane season.

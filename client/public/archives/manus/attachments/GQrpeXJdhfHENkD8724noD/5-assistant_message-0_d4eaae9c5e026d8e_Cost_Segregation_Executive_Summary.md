## 1. Executive Overview

This report evaluates the recent Cost Segregation Studies conducted by CSSI (Cost Segregation Services LLC) across eight (8) properties within the Apartment Corp portfolio. 

The primary objective of these studies is to **accelerate depreciation deductions** by reclassifying assets from standard 27.5-year straight-line real property into shorter MACRS recovery periods (5-year, 7-year, and 15-year property). This strategic tax planning tool significantly increases upfront cash flow by deferring tax liabilities.

### Portfolio Impact Summary
Based on our analysis of the total **$33.05 Million** in project costs reviewed across the 8 properties:
* **$9.11 Million (27.6%)** of property was successfully reclassified into accelerated depreciation categories (5, 7, and 15-year MACRS).
* **Additional Year 1 Depreciation:** The studies generated an estimated **$1.26 Million** in *additional* first-year depreciation deductions compared to the straight-line baseline.
* **5-Year Cumulative Benefit:** Over the first 5 years, the accelerated depreciation will yield an estimated **$6.29 Million** in additional tax deductions.

## 4. IRS Form 3115 & Section 481(a) Adjustments

Because some of these properties were placed in service in prior tax years, implementing the cost segregation findings requires a formal change in accounting method. This is achieved by filing **IRS Form 3115 (Application for Change in Accounting Method)**.

This process triggers a **Section 481(a) adjustment**, which allows the taxpayer to "catch up" on the depreciation that *could have been claimed* in prior years had the cost segregation study been done originally. A negative §481(a) adjustment represents a favorable decrease in taxable income.

Based on the documentation provided, CSSI has prepared Form 3115s and calculated the catch-up adjustments for the following properties:

1. **Grove Park Housing, LP:** Total negative §481(a) adjustment of **($257,134.54)**.
   * *This includes the site, office, 5 residential buildings, and apartment improvements.*
2. **Grace Townhomes Housing, LLC:** Total negative §481(a) adjustment of **($120,565.76)**.
   * *This includes the site, clubhouse, storage, and 25 residential buildings.*

**Accounting Note on ADS Election:**
Communications with the CPA (Jeff Sedacca) indicate that several entities (including St. Charles Place and Silver Springs Terrace) have made **IRC Section 163(j) elections**, which limit business interest deductions but *require* the entities to use the Alternative Depreciation System (ADS). ADS mandates longer recovery periods and straight-line depreciation, which slightly alters the standard MACRS calculations. The accounting department must ensure the cost segregation data is properly integrated with these ADS requirements.

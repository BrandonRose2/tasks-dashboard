## [SCENE 1: THE PROBLEM – LEAKING MONEY]

**VISUAL:**
We open on a sleek, modern property management office. The lighting is slightly dim, emphasizing the glow of computer screens. A property manager looks stressed, surrounded by sticky notes and ringing phones. 
*Quick, dynamic cuts:* A missed call on a smartphone. A leaky pipe in an apartment. A frustrated tenant. A spreadsheet filled with red numbers.

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
*(Deep, confident, empathetic)*
"Most property owners don't know where the money is disappearing. They just know it is. Missed leasing calls. Forgotten repairs that turn into expensive emergencies. Hours of manual labor. It’s not just inefficiency—it’s leaking revenue."

**VISUAL:**
The camera pushes in on a spreadsheet. The numbers blur and transition into a sleek, dark-mode digital interface. The PropertyMax.ai logo fades in elegantly: *PropertyMax.ai – AI Operating Layer for Real Estate.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
"Enter PropertyMax.ai. Not just another software. The AI Operating System for property management."

Case Summary

Washington City Police (Utah) arrested three California residents on January 17, 2018, in connection with an identity fraud and theft operation. The suspects allegedly rented vacation property in Washington City using a fraudulently obtained credit card through a local property management company.

Search warrants were executed at a Washington City home, a rental property in Santa Clara, and a fraudulently rented vehicle. Officers seized a large quantity of fraudulent driver's licenses, financial transaction cards, other financial documents, stolen property, and equipment used to manufacture fraudulent documents.

Washington City Police stated the investigation is ongoing with local, state, and federal agency coordination, and may involve incidents throughout Utah and other states. Police encouraged residents to secure mail, credit cards, checkbooks, and identification.

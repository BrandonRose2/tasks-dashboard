Pro Tips

Send this email to ALL 10 dealers at the same time. Let them compete against each other.
Don't visit the lot first. Once you're physically there, you lose all leverage.
"Out-the-door" forces them to include ALL fees upfront — no surprises at signing.
Say NO to dealer add-ons (nitrogen tires, paint protection, LoJack). They charge $2K-$4K for junk.
If they say "market adjustment" — walk. No supply shortage in 2026 justifies a markup.
End of month = better deals. Salespeople have monthly quotas — use that pressure.
Once you get the lowest quote, forward it to other dealers: "Can you beat $X OTD?"
Check Costco Auto Program if you have a membership — guaranteed no-haggle below MSRP.
Don't buy extended warranty at signing. You can buy from ANY Toyota dealer later for less.

Phone Excellence

Phone Excellence is not a suggestion. It is the minimum standard for every call, every time, with every caller.

The Four Pillars of Phone Excellence

Every call must reflect all four pillars—no exceptions. These standards apply equally to a frustrated tenant and an excited prospect. There is no call too short or too routine to skip them.

Pillar 01

Professionalism

Clear, composed, timely communication. Every commitment made on a call must be followed through. You represent ApartmentCorp on every call.

  • Answer within 3 rings.
  • Introduce yourself by name.
Pillar 02

Warmth

Callers must feel genuinely welcomed and valued. Active listening and empathetic responses are non-negotiable. Warmth is demonstrated through action.

  • Use the caller's name.
  • Acknowledge before answering.
Pillar 03

Consistency

Every caller receives the same high-quality experience regardless of time, call type, or which staff member answers. Consistency builds trust at scale.

  • Same standard every call.
  • No exceptions for "easy" calls.
Pillar 04

Accuracy

Information captured correctly and completely on the first attempt. Accurate data powers PropertyMax.ai's AI systems. Wrong data breaks the workflow.

  • Verify every data point.
  • Accuracy is a system requirement.

Professionalism in Practice

Professionalism is not about formality—it is about reliability. When you answer a call, you represent ApartmentCorp.

Do Don't
Answer within 3 rings
Prompt answers signal that callers are a priority. 		Let calls go to voicemail during business hours
Missed calls = missed prospects and frustrated tenants.
Introduce yourself by name and property
"Thank you for calling [Property], this is [Name]" 		Answer with just "hello" or "yeah?"
Callers don't know who they reached or if they called the right place.
Follow through on every commitment made
If you say you'll call back by 3pm, call back by 3pm. 		Promise a callback and forget
Broken commitments destroy trust and generate complaint calls.
Stay composed and solution-focused
Calm, clear responses de-escalate difficult callers. 		Match the caller's frustration or become defensive
Emotional reactions escalate situations and reflect poorly on the property.

Warmth: Active Listening Is a Skill

Warmth means making the caller feel heard. This requires active listening—repeating back key information, acknowledging concerns before jumping to solutions, and using the caller's name throughout the conversation.

Acknowledge First

Before offering any solution or information, acknowledge what the caller has said. This signals that you are listening.

Say This

"I understand — let me make sure I have this right before we move forward…"

Use Their Name

Personalization signals that this is not a scripted, transactional interaction. Using the caller's name makes the conversation feel individual.

Say This

"Absolutely, Maria — let me pull that up for you right now."

Confirm Understanding

Before answering or transferring, confirm that you have understood the caller's need correctly. This prevents miscommunication.

Say This

"So what you're looking for is a 2-bedroom available in March — is that right?"

Accuracy Drives the AI: Garbage In, Garbage Out

PropertyMax.ai's AI features—automatic prospect records, AI-generated To-Do's, call summaries, and follow-up suggestions—are only as good as the data that feeds them.

The Accuracy Rule: Accuracy is not just good practice—it is a system requirement. Every field you fill in correctly is a step in the AI chain. Every field you skip or get wrong breaks the chain—and costs a lease.

The 5 Golden Questions

These five questions are the minimum information required from every prospect on every call. Missing even one creates a gap in the prospect record that the AI cannot fill. These are not a script—they are a checklist.

# Question to Ask Why It Matters
01 What is your full name? Personalized communication + accurate prospect record creation in PropertyMax.ai.
02 What is the best phone number to reach you? Primary follow-up contact method—required for AI-generated To-Do's and callbacks.
03 What is your email address? Tour confirmations, property brochures, lease application links, and follow-up communications.
04 What is your reason for calling today? Understand needs, tailor the conversation, and populate the AI-generated call summary correctly.
05 How did you hear about us? Marketing channel effectiveness data—directly informs where advertising dollars are spent.

Critical Rule: All 5 questions must be asked on every prospect call—even if the call is short, even if the prospect seems impatient, even if you already know their name.

The Red X Rule: Close It Out — Every Time

Pressing the red X is how you close out completed items and keep the dashboard clean. It appears in three places. If it's not closed, it's still your responsibility.

Location 01

Call Recordings

Press the Red X only after you have:

  • Listened to the full recording.
  • Verified the AI-generated To-Do's are accurate and assigned.
  • Confirmed the prospect record in the CRM is complete.
  • Sent any required follow-up text or email through the platform.
Location 02

Maintenance Requests

Press the Red X only after you have:

  • Confirmed the work is physically done.
  • Called or texted the tenant to verify they are satisfied with the repair.
  • Logged any follow-up actions if the repair was incomplete.
Location 03

Tickets

Press the Red X only after you have:

  • Read the full ticket.
  • Taken the required action.
  • Followed up on sent tickets open for more than 24 hours.
  • Escalated to regional manager if open for 2+ days.

The Daily Morning Routine

Every reactive day starts with a skipped morning routine. These six steps in order ensure nothing falls through the cracks—every tenant is heard, every request is assigned, every follow-up is sent before the phones start ringing.

01. Collections

Review all outstanding rent balances. Flag accounts that are 3+ days past due. Send a reminder text through the platform. Log every action in the record.

02. Call Recordings

Open the 5 tabs. Review every unreviewed call. Complete all action items. Press the Red X on each reviewed recording.

03. To-Do's

Work through all open To-Do's. Complete each task, add notes, and close completed items. Do not carry yesterday's To-Do's into tomorrow.

04. Maintenance

Check Unassigned first—assign every one before moving on. Then check Overdue. Zero unassigned requests by end of morning routine.

05. Tickets

Check Tickets Received—read and action each one. Check Sent Tickets—follow up on anything older than 24 hours with no response.

06. Text Messages

Reply to every unanswered text from tenants and prospects. No message should go unanswered past the start of your shift.

Section 2: Collections Overview

The Collections section gives you a real-time financial snapshot of your property. It shows four key numbers—and the relationship between them tells you exactly where your attention is needed today.

Gates

Expected total rent for the property. This is your baseline.

Watch For

Sudden drops may indicate a lease expiration or vacancy that wasn't updated.

Billed

Amount actually charged to tenants. It should match Gates closely.

Watch For

Billed significantly lower than Gates = missed charges or posting errors.

Collected

Amount actually received from tenants. This is your actual revenue.

Watch For

Low Collected relative to Billed = active delinquency requiring follow-up calls.

Difference

The gap between Billed and Collected. A large Difference is a list of tenants who need to be contacted today.

Action Required

Any significant Difference triggers immediate follow-up—calls, texts, and To-Do's.

Section 3: To-Do's — Your Task Management Center

The To-Do's section is your daily task engine. It has four tabs—each representing a different category of follow-up work. Review all four every shift, address overdue items first, and never let a task live only in your head.

Tab 01

My To-Do's

Your personal task list—everything assigned directly to you. This is your starting point every shift. Overdue items appear at the top and must be addressed before anything else.

  • Overdue first—any item past its due date is a priority.
  • AI-generated tasks from call recordings appear here automatically.
  • Mark complete immediately after action—do not leave tasks open.
Tab 02

Rent

Your collections follow-up engine. Every item in the Rent tab represents a direct revenue impact. Missing a Rent To-Do is not just a task failure—it is a financial failure.

  • Cross-reference with the Collections section Difference number.
  • Every delinquent tenant should have an active Rent To-Do.
  • Log outcome of every follow-up call or text in the record.
Tab 03

Sales

Your leasing pipeline management tool. Every prospect, tour, and application should generate a Sales To-Do. This tab keeps the leasing funnel moving from first call to signed lease.

  • Follow up with prospects within 24 hours of their call.
  • Tour confirmation and post-tour follow-up tasks appear here.
  • Application status updates and approval follow-ups tracked here.
Tab 04

Maintenance

Work order follow-up tasks that directly affect resident retention. A maintenance request that goes unacknowledged is one of the top reasons tenants choose not to renew their lease.

  • Cross-reference with the Maintenance Requests section daily.
  • Every Unassigned request should generate a Maintenance To-Do.
  • Update tenant on status—even a "still working on it" call matters.

Section 6: Section 8 Inspections

Section 8 inspections are HUD-mandated—missing or delaying one puts the property's housing assistance contract at risk. Every inspection moves through three statuses: Pending, Scheduled, and Completed.

Status 01

Pending

Every unscheduled inspection in the Pending column is a compliance risk. Check it before anything else in your morning routine.

Call or text the tenant to schedule access immediately.

Status 02

Scheduled

Send a confirmation text and call the tenant the day before the inspection. No-shows are almost always a failure to confirm.

Have the inspection checklist and unit history ready.

Status 03

Completed

After the inspection, open the record and click Complete Checklist. This updates the status and triggers follow-up actions.

Record whether the inspection passed, required repairs, or failed.

08

Section 8: Maintenance Requests

Maintenance Requests must be worked in priority order—Unassigned first, then Overdue, then Assigned. Every request that goes unacknowledged is a resident who feels ignored and a lease renewal at risk.

01. Unassigned

Unassigned requests mean a resident has no one working their issue. Assign to a vendor or tech before moving on.

02. Overdue

Past expected completion—contact the vendor or tech immediately and update the tenant with a new date.

03. Assigned

Work is in progress. Check in with the vendor if the date is near and proactively update the tenant.

04. Completed

Confirm the work is done before closing. Call or text the tenant to verify satisfaction, then close the record.

Section 9: Tickets

Tickets are your internal communication channel with leadership and the regional team—not a tool for tenant issues. Use Tickets when you need a decision, a reimbursement, tech support, or to escalate a staffing matter.

Accountability Rule: Every ticket you submit is your responsibility until it is resolved and closed. "I submitted it" is not the same as "it was handled." Check Sent Tickets every day. If a ticket has no response after 24 hours, follow up. After 48 hours, contact the regional manager directly.

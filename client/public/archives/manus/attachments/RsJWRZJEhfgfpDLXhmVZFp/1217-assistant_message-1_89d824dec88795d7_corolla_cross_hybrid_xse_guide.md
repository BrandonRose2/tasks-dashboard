## Pro Tips for Your Friend

1. **Send this email to ALL 10 dealers at the same time.** Let them compete.
2. **Don't visit the lot first.** Once you're physically there, you lose leverage.
3. **"Out-the-door" is the magic phrase.** It forces them to include ALL fees upfront — no surprises.
4. **Say "no dealer add-ons."** Many dealers pre-install nitrogen tires, paint protection, LoJack, etc. and charge $2,000–$4,000 extra. Refuse all of it.
5. **If they say "market adjustment" — walk.** In 2026, there's no supply shortage justifying a markup on this car.
6. **Use Costco Auto Program** if you have a membership — guaranteed no-haggle pricing below MSRP at participating dealers.
7. **End of month = better deals.** Salespeople have monthly quotas. Last week of the month gives you more leverage.
8. **Once you get the lowest quote, send it to the other dealers:** "I have a quote for $X OTD from another dealer. Can you beat it?" Let them fight.
9. **Check TrueCar/Edmunds** for the "fair purchase price" in your area as a benchmark.
10. **Don't buy the extended warranty at signing.** If you want one later, buy it from any Toyota dealer — you don't have to buy it where you purchased the car.

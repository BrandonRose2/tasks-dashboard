# Enzo Dialer: Public Product Findings

**Source reviewed:** https://enzodialer.com/ on 2026-08-31.

## Positioning

Enzo Dialer describes itself as a cloud outbound dialer built for real-estate teams. It emphasizes increasing conversations from the same lead list and runs dialing on its own carrier infrastructure.

## Publicly advertised capabilities

| Capability | Public description |
|---|---|
| Dialing modes | Power, preview, and predictive dialing on a unified platform. |
| Parallel dialing | Up to 14 lines per agent on Standard plans; shared/poolable lines. Enterprise has no published cap. |
| Caller ID management | 35 caller IDs per seat on Starter, 100 per seat on Standard and above; number rotation, reputation monitoring, local/regional presence, optional CNAM. |
| CRM workflows | Native bidirectional sync with Follow Up Boss: outcomes push to FUB and contacts/stages pull back. Other CRMs connect one-way via Zapier/webhooks, including GoHighLevel, Salesforce, and HubSpot. |
| Supervisor controls | Live floor view; listen, whisper, and barge; inbound routing for blended teams. |
| Agent workspace and reporting | Live manager/agent dashboards, call recordings with storage, scripts, dispositions, and agent-ranked call distribution. |
| Campaign/list management | CSV import, deduplication, time-zone-aware calling windows, daily limits, and campaign-level do-not-call controls. |
| Compliance boundary stated by vendor | Campaign DNC is handled in-product, but national registry scrubbing is explicitly left to the customer's list provider. |
| Pricing/unit economics claims | Site states that inbound and outbound minutes are included on Starter and Standard, and a sub-3% drop rate is claimed at about 30 contacts/hour on Tier-1 carriers. These claims require independent validation before comparison. |

## Practical interpretation for rental vacancies

A comparable internal product should focus first on lead intake, single-line click-to-call or preview dialing, qualified leasing dispositions, follow-up scheduling, property/unit context, compliant opt-out controls, recordings, manager reporting, and reliable CRM synchronization. High-volume predictive dialing and caller-ID rotation are technically feasible but should be deferred until call-consent rules, abandonment controls, operating volume, and carrier approval are established.

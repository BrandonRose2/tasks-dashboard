## 2. AI Systems & Automation Workflows
Apartment Corp utilizes several AI and automation tools to manage their properties and staff:

### Manus AI Integration
Manus AI ($29/month) is used as the central AI software for operations. It connects to various systems to automate complex manager performance workflows.

### Operations Hub & KPI Tracking
The company uses a "PropertyMax.ai V2.0 : Operations Hub" (hosted at operationshub-aptcorp.manus.space) to track performance. 
- Managers are evaluated based on KPI scores.
- The system tracks metrics like leads, appointments, listings, and closings.
- There is a specific focus on "Phone Excellence" and capturing required information on prospect calls (the "5 Golden Questions").

### Vacancy Report App
A dedicated "Vacancy Report App" (hosted at apartmentcorpvacancyreport.manus.space) is used to monitor property vacancies across the portfolio.

### Automated Coaching Workflow
The AI system (Manus) connects these data sources to automate management oversight:
1. **Data Retrieval**: The LLM pulls real-time KPI data from the Operations Hub and live occupancy data from the Vacancy Report App.
2. **Performance Evaluation**: It autonomously identifies underperforming managers based on these metrics.
3. **Reporting**: It generates detailed Excel reports regarding property vacancies.
4. **Action & Communication**: The process culminates in the AI drafting personalized constructive coaching emails to the underperforming managers.
5. **Scheduling**: It automatically schedules follow-up meetings in the calendar to address specific operational weaknesses.

## 3. Manager Contact & Tracking
The "Managers & Assistants : Contact List" database tracks all property managers across 5 regions. It monitors:

- Training Status (Date Trained, Scheduled Training)
- Payroll and AP information
- Property assignments and contact details

## 4. Training & Development
The "PropertyMax.ai Training Hub" includes modules on:
- Phone Excellence
- Marketing Training
- Call Recordings
- CRM Management (Prospects, Tenants, Vendors)
- Maintenance Requests

Staff are incentivized with prizes for the most conversions, highest KPI scores on the "5 Golden Questions", and highest completion of To-Do items.
